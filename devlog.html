LOG 05 · Sep 13, 2026 · 5 min

Making Survival Matter

The early food loop gets a big overhaul — Food Fabricator becomes an emergency fallback, Hydroponics Bay ties long-term survival to a real supply line, plus a continued pixel-art UI pass across the whole interface.

 LOG 04 · Dec 13, 2025 · 3 min

Quest System Planning

A goal-driven progression layer for the colony's next phase. Objectives now unlock new buildings as rewards, replacing idle waiting with a clear sense of progress.

 LOG 03 · Nov 16, 2025 · 4 min

Food & Survival System

Colonist survival stats turn the stockpile into a meaningful decision. Stronghold-style food mechanics track hunger per colonist, so a shortage has real consequences.

 LOG 02 · Nov 15, 2025 · 3 min

Oxygen Consumption System

Pressure zones and aluminium upkeep create immediate environmental risk. Buildings now consume oxygen locally, so a single failure can cascade fast.

Created a comprehensive design document to establish the game's vision, core mechanics, and technical architecture before building further.
