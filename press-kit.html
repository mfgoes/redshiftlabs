Overview

The short version

Kosmograd puts the player in command of a Soviet lunar colony. Build the systems that keep it alive while managing resource scarcity, survival, and political pressure from Earth.

Oxygen, food, and power are not background numbers: failing equipment and a hostile lunar environment turn every expansion decision into a risk. It combines base-building strategy with the tension of a survival sim in a Space Race-era setting.

Mission systems

Key features

  • Survival systems: balance oxygen, food, water, and power.
  • Dynamic failures: respond to shortages, equipment faults, and hazards.
  • Connected construction: build and expand a lunar colony.
  • Individual colonists: each crew member has needs and survival stats.
  • Political pressure: keep the colony stable under directives from Earth.
